# Executive Memo: FY2025 CIB Performance & Expense Analytics

**To:** CIB Management  
**From:** Performance Analytics  
**Subject:** FY2025 performance, expense recovery, and reporting-control observations  
**Currency:** CAD  
**Data:** Fully synthetic portfolio demonstration

## Executive Summary

FY2025 revenue reached **$197.7M**, which was **$9.4M, or 4.5%, below plan**. Net contribution was **$165.5M** after **$32.2M** of direct and allocated expense, producing an **83.7% net contribution margin** under the simplified portfolio methodology.

The largest performance pressure came from **Equity Capital Markets**, which finished approximately **$10.6M below plan**. Debt Capital Markets exceeded plan by approximately **$4.1M**, partially offsetting ECM and Corporate Lending weakness. The five largest clients generated **29.3%** of revenue, indicating moderate concentration rather than dependence on a single relationship.

Expense recovery was **84.8%**. Of **$17.0M** recoverable expense, **$14.4M** had been recovered and **$2.6M** remained outstanding. There were **163 delayed records**, with an average delay of **57.4 days**. Northstar Energy had the largest client-level unrecovered balance at approximately **$300.7K**.

The data-quality log contained **120 issues**. **49** remained open or in remediation, including **four critical issues**, while unresolved financial differences totaled approximately **$578.4K**. Missing definitions and unreconciled amounts were the most frequent categories.

## Recommended Management Actions

1. **Investigate ECM underperformance.** Review pipeline conversion, execution timing, fee realization, and client/product segments contributing the largest shortfalls.
2. **Prioritize material recovery items.** Introduce escalation thresholds based on balance, aging, and client materiality; assign owners to the largest client and vendor exposures.
3. **Review capital deployment.** Assess client revenue and contribution together with average capital, risk, and broader relationship value before changing coverage or limits.
4. **Tighten DQ remediation governance.** Establish weekly owner-level tracking for critical and high-severity issues, with due dates and escalation triggers.
5. **Reconcile before publication.** Require material source-system differences and ledger/reporting breaks to be resolved or formally explained before management reporting release.

## Limitations

The dataset is fully synthetic. Net contribution is not full economic profit and excludes compensation, funding costs, tax, expected loss, and other costs. Average capital is a simplified monthly average. The DQ table is an issue log, not a complete tested population, so a data-quality pass rate cannot be inferred.
